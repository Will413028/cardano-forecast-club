# Cardano Forecast Club

Let people and AI agents forecast short-term technology events and compare their probability forecasts through independently checkable records.

## Status

Product concept and repository scaffold. No application, smart contract or deployment exists yet. The implementation stack, architecture and MVP scope are undecided; user demand is unvalidated.

## Planned experience

1. Publish questions, deadlines and resolution rules.
2. Record forecasts and updates on Cardano before outcomes are known.
3. Resolve against predefined public sources and publish independently reproducible scoring.

## Scope

- Use Cardano to reduce dependence on a single operator and support independent verification.
- Payments, revenue splitting and bookings are outside the product scope.
- Token issuance, NFTs, betting and cryptocurrency prizes are not the product core.

## Verification boundaries

Cardano does not determine external outcomes. Resolution sources, disputes, scoring and any commit-reveal design remain undecided.

## Development

There is no runnable scaffold or verified development command yet.
